# Cold Outreach Lists: Roofing + HVAC

| File | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `roofing_contacts_100.csv` | 100 US roofing companies: Instagram handle, phone (89), email (37), website (90) |
| `hvac_contacts_100.csv` | 100 US HVAC companies: Instagram handle for DMs (phone where public) |

All contacts came from public business listings (Instagram profiles, company sites, BBB/Yelp/GAF pages) found in September 2026.
Open either CSV in Google Sheets or Excel. Update the `Status`, `Date Contacted` and `Follow-up Date` columns as you work through the list.

**Before sending:** check each Instagram handle once (accounts get renamed or deleted), and personalize the first line using something from their recent posts.

---

## Replace these placeholders first

- `[Your Name]`: your name
- `[Offer]`: what you're selling, in one line (e.g. "we get roofers 10–20 booked inspections a month with paid ads")
- `[Proof]`: one result (e.g. "helped a Phoenix roofer add $80k in jobs last quarter")
- `{Company}`, `{City}`: take these from the CSV

## Instagram DMs (roofing and HVAC)

**DM 1: opener (keep it short, no pitch)**
> Hey {Company} team, came across your page while looking at {roofers / HVAC companies} in {City}. That last job you posted looks great. Quick question: are you the owner, or is there someone better to talk to about growing the business?

**DM 2: after they reply**
> Appreciate it! I'm [Your Name]. [Offer]. We recently [Proof]. Would you be open to a 10-minute call this week to see if it fits {Company}?

**DM 3: follow-up after 2–3 days with no reply**
> Just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to send a quick 60-second video showing how it'd work for {Company} if that's easier.

**DM 4: last message after 5–7 more days**
> Last note from me. If more {roof inspections / service calls} isn't a priority right now, no worries. Want me to check back next season?

HVAC timing: pitch before peak season (late spring for AC, early fall for heating).

## Text/SMS (roofing, phone numbers in the CSV)

> Hi, is this {Company}? This is [Your Name]. I found you on Instagram. [Offer, one line]. Open to a quick call this week? Reply STOP to opt out.

---

## Staying compliant

- **Email (CAN-SPAM):** use an honest subject line, include your physical mailing address and a working opt-out, and honor opt-outs within 10 business days.
- **Texting (TCPA):** most of these numbers are business main lines, and some may be cell phones. Sending cold marketing texts with an automated or bulk texting tool without prior consent carries legal risk. Texts typed by hand one at a time are safer. Always identify yourself and include "Reply STOP to opt out." Calling first is the lowest-risk option.
- **Instagram:** send about 20–30 cold DMs per day per account and vary the wording. Sending the same message in bulk can get the account restricted.
